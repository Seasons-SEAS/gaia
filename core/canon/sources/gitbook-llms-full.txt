# Introduction

High-level Introduction to Season's

Seasons is the autonomous, internet-native, tokenized liquidity system designed to facilitate the generation, distribution, and accumulation of yield through a polymer of modern DEFI mechanisms and a novel adaptation of predecessor mechanisms that leverage a combination of economic and social variables to derive, capture, and circulate value. An [operator-owned protocol](/seasons-docs/protocol/nodes) accessible only to the $SEAS token holder network.&#x20;

***

### Guiding Principles

Inspired by nature, Seasons applies 4 guiding principles across all product design considerations.

{% columns %}
{% column %}

#### **🟪 Resilience**&#x20;

> Capable of withstanding any potentially critical issues without operational impacts.&#x20;
> {% endcolumn %}

{% column %}

#### **🟪 Scalability**

> Capable of accommodating any number of users without deteriorating the user experience or system efficiency.
> {% endcolumn %}
> {% endcolumns %}

{% columns %}
{% column %}

#### **🟪 Permissionless**

> Always liquid. Non-custodial. No counterparty dependencies.

{% endcolumn %}

{% column %}

#### **🟪 Efficiency**

> Reflexivity elements embedded into the core logic to ensure proportionality  in system's growth sustainibly.&#x20;
> {% endcolumn %}
> {% endcolumns %}

***

### Assumptions

Seasons has been designed according to a multitude of "high-probability" outcomes of game-theory simulations, which indicate that in non-controlled environments (mainnet), operator behavior can veer dramatically. Assumptions apply to a supermajority (\~67% or 2/3) of users, approximately 80% of the time.

{% columns %}
{% column %}

#### **〰️ Extractive Nature of Operators**

> Seasons assumes that the supermajority of operators will act in accordance to rational actor theory and imply constant strain on the system in order to recoup their allocation(s).
> {% endcolumn %}

{% column %}

#### **〰️ Long Time Preference**

> Seasons assumes that each new incoming operator shall maintain their position for no less than 90 days.
> {% endcolumn %}
> {% endcolumns %}

{% columns %}
{% column %}

#### **〰️ Agentic User Evolution**

> Seasons assumes that the very definition of a user is radically evolving with the advent of AI. Users are getting more sophisticated and, in turn, demand broader capabilities.
> {% endcolumn %}

{% column %}

#### **〰️ Infinite Vol**

> Seasons assumes that volatility across all assets and geographies will continue to increase; thus, more people will increasingly need more reliability and diversity in their selections.
> {% endcolumn %}
> {% endcolumns %}

***

### Why Seasons

Seasons' Yield 3.0 model solves one of the yield industry's greatest conundrums: sustainability for perpetual scale. Predecessor models are beholden to deteriorating rates as a project's/platform's liquidity/size grows; laws of physics applied to finance as it relates to sourcing yield. Seasons synthesizes and extends the financial primitives of traditional yield mechanisms by embedding reflexive technical features and social factors to generate "boundless/non-linear" real yield through a transfer-fee implementation.

Moreover, heavily set in consideration around a burgeoning agentic internet economy, the Yield 3.0 system's embedded reflexivity features, digitally native form factor, and core output model (assets + distributions) unlocks scalable, progressive sustainability; becoming appealing for agentic users.

# YMS

Yield Management System

{% hint style="warning" %}
Seasons' yield system is in its ***alpha*** experimentation phase, being actively modified/optimized for sustainability. While no fundamental changes are anticipated, adjustments to components like the weight balancing function, distribution model, transactional tax, etc., are likely with each progressive iteration of the product.
{% endhint %}

***

### Overview

The **YMS** or **Y**ield **M**anagement **S**ystem is the orchestration layer of the SEASONS Yield 3.0 mechanism. It maps, coordinates, and sequences logical circuits across/between all three of the modules of the yield mechanism and their concurrent system components, namely Yield Accumulation and Yield Distribution.

The YMS is Seasons' "Data-Set Authority", playing a role at every critical junction in the flow of liquidity throughout the Seasons mechanism. In addition to monitoring the state of seasons in real time, detecting anomalies and deterring potential disruptions, it acts as a pseudo-oracle, ensuring consistency and continuity of operations.&#x20;

***

#### &#x20;**🔄️️Yield Accumulation**

Composed of three (3) distinct modules for generating and capturing value, the yield mechanism operates from a *risk-first* perspective, where each independent constituent module optimizes for its preservation, providing a stable, reliable, and consistent flow of revenue based on asset retention. The modules are further complemented by a reflexive and unit accumulation component that ensures steady, persistent growth over juiced short-term returns.

{% hint style="success" icon="triple-chevrons-right" %}
**Learn more about:**  [<kbd><mark style="color:blue;">**`Yield Accumlation`**<mark style="color:blue;"></kbd>](/seasons-docs/protocol/yield-3.0-mechanism/generation)
{% endhint %}

#### **🛜 Yield Distribution**

Referring to the transformation of proceeds into revenue that ultimately arrives in the hands of node operators, Season yield distribution is a multi-functional process consisting of harvesting proceeds from each module, consolidating them in an "acquirer," swapping for payout assets, and correctly disbursing them through a "distributor."

{% hint style="success" icon="triple-chevrons-right" %}
**Learn more about:**  [<kbd><mark style="color:blue;">**`Yield Distribution`**<mark style="color:blue;"></kbd>](/seasons-docs/protocol/yield-3.0-mechanism/distribution)
{% endhint %}

#### **🧭 Router State Validation**

Maintaining the State of Seasons Node Network (Director and Builder) requires multiple rounds of assurance checking (liveliness). Enforcing the criteria requirements and liquidity makes the YMS responsible for routing capital by ascribing which addresses are tentatively qualified for inclusion in the nearest future distribution.

{% hint style="success" icon="triple-chevrons-right" %}
**Learn more about:**  [<kbd><mark style="color:blue;">**`Nodes`**<mark style="color:blue;"></kbd>](/seasons-docs/protocol/nodes)
{% endhint %}

#### **⚖️ Data-Set Authority**

In order for each component of the Yield 3.0 mechanism to function, an enforcement of algorithmic compliance against an irrefutable source of truth must be present. The YMS actively feeds all operational trust variables into Seasons, becoming the final directive on how much gets distributed at any given distribution, what assets are acquired for distribution, in what proportion and to where they are distributed, how the balance of the SSYM is utilized, when the YAV realizes its efforts to boost POL, and everything else formulaic in between.

# TTT

Transactional Transfer Tax

{% hint style="success" %}
**Active**\
*The TTT is the first module in the Seasons Yield 3.0 system by order of implementation. Going live immediately at launch, the TTT drives the majority of the output during the bootstrapping phase of the full mechanism.*
{% endhint %}

***

### Overview

Transactional Transfer Tax (TTT) is a core value-generation and accrual module of Seasons' [Yield 3.0 mechanism](/seasons-docs/protocol/yield-3.0-mechanism), built from first principles of the digital-native internet economy.

<figure><img src="https://714818086-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2FLy6pIqxkZG5ykeDMj55p%2Fuploads%2Fcofubrue3IhVVr8TcEsg%2FTTT2.png?alt=media&amp;token=8eab1705-a0da-4a25-bad3-d3146e281233" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The TTT logic is embedded in the [$SEAS token](/seasons-docs/protocol/seas-token)'s smart contract, imposing a fee on every buy, sell, and transfer. Accumulated fees are directly swapped into assets from the current [Inclusion Set](/seasons-docs/protocol/yield-3.0-mechanism/distribution/inclusion-lists) and distributed as yield to all active [nodes](/seasons-docs/protocol/nodes).&#x20;

Every on-chain movement of the $SEAS token — including transfers and trades on decentralized exchanges like [Jupiter](https://jup.ag/tokens/7GdpaeSzvkx1a78rRkU11KstM1x8naMmMmmpWQnQSEAS) — invokes the transactional tax (txTAX), capturing a portion of the total trade size as follows:&#x20;

* **Entry Position ('Buy' Trade):** 10% of the USD equivalent of the *payment asset* at the moment of transaction, accounted for and collected in $SEAS.
* **Exit Position ('Sell' Trade):** 10% of the USD equivalent of $SEAS at the moment of transaction.&#x20;

Based on this txTAX component, the formula for deriving yield is directly related to real economic activity involving $SEAS, and can be defined as:&#x20;

> `(Volume + txTAX) x Velocity`

{% hint style="info" %}
Volume, Velocity, and txTAX are defined and explained in more details below.
{% endhint %}

***

### The Three Components of TTT

Three components/characteristics capture the essence of TTT:

* **Transactional Tax (txTAX):** Transaction-fee-based value accrual mechanism. Use, pay, profit.&#x20;
* **Volume:** Total value flowing through Seasons' Yield 3.0 architecture and $SEAS tokens over time. More volume → More yield.&#x20;
* **Velocity:** Speed or frequency at which $SEAS (or other assets in the system) switch hands. Reduces noise and enables high-quality volume.&#x20;

While each fulfill its unique purpose, these components facilitate the *sourcing, generation, retention*, and *distribution* of value through Seasons.&#x20;

#### Transactional Tax (txTAX)

Seasons' fee mechanism — a.k.a. txTAX — is the kernel and starting point of Yield 3.0 model.&#x20;

> **The txTAX component imposes a straightforward 10% fee upon entering (purchase) and exiting (sale) a $SEAS position.**&#x20;

{% hint style="success" icon="corn" %}
Example: Say, User A buys $SEAS worth 100 USDC. With the 10% txTAX, they receive $SEAS worth \~90 USDC. Likewise, when selling 100 $SEAS, they receive USDC worth 90 $SEAS. &#x20;
{% endhint %}

By taxing every transaction, Seasons ensures long-term interest alignment for holders. The txTAX deters actors with low time preference or extractive intent. Whereas by incurring a small cost in the form of a contribution to the yield-generation mechanism, aligned participants stimulate value flow and become qualified to earn from the system.&#x20;

The TTT module's APY is thus comprised of the txTAX accumulated from every BUY and SELL operation involving $SEAS, accounted for and collected in real time. And to ensure equality for all participants, Seasons' imposes a unilateral txTAX regardless of transaction size or frequency.

#### Volume & Velocity — The 'Double Vs'

Coupled with txTAX, the 'Double Vs' — Volume and Velocity — drive Seasons' TTT module.&#x20;

The oxygen for financial markets, volume is the combination of an asset's price and its trading frequency within a specific time period. Being highly reflexive in nature, *volume begets more volume*. When a given asset or trading pair has high volume, it generally commands more interest/demand. On the contrary, when the volume fades, demand typically fades as well.&#x20;

Based on this dynamic, Seasons' mechanism is designed with volume as a core parameter facilitating yield-generation within the TTT module. It's a linear relationship, where:&#x20;

> `Higher Volume → Higher Yield. Lower Volume → Lower Yield.`&#x20;

Then there's velocity, i.e., the parameter used to gauge the actual interest/demand derived from the volume component.&#x20;

Velocity refers to the frequency at which a given asset — $SEAS or otherwise — switches owners (or, how many times the same address touches an asset within a given timeframe). It has an inverse relationship with ownership duration:&#x20;

* Low velocity signals long-term ownership and low time preference.
* High velocity means shorter ownership duration and high time preference.

Seasons explicitly leverages the velocity component as an 'auto-immune' or defence mechanism, deterring mercenarial, malicious, or otherwise extractive entities. Coupled with the txTAX component that reduces noise to derive a more honest (and sustainable) APY, velocity dissuades market makers and similar entities from attempting market-distorting coups.&#x20;

The `(Volume + txTAX) x Velocity` approach thus allows Seasons to serve long-term market interests. And unlike superfluous projects/products that fake their volume and other metrics, Seasons optimizes for *sustainability* by maximizing *organic volume based on organic demand*.&#x20;

<mark style="background-color:yellow;">Asset distributions are proportionate.</mark>

***

### **Tax Proceed Utilization**

Proceeds collected through taxation are denominated in $SEAS and accumulated in the **Acquirer Escrow** address(es). These addresses hold the tokens until they receive an engagement signal, at which point the accumulated $SEAS is transferred to a **Distributor** address.

The Distributor contract/address swaps the $SEAS for assets defined in the current Inclusion Set at the time of distribution. After acquisition, the Distributor contract awaits its trigger to disseminate the acquired assets to qualified wallet addresses ([Seasons Nodes](/seasons-docs/protocol/nodes)).&#x20;

***

### **TTT Yield Distribution**

The distribution/payout modality for fee-based yield is one of Seasons' definitive features.&#x20;

Traditionally, yield is paid in the same asset that the user holds, stakes, or spends to acquire the yield-bearing instrument. For example, they get more ETH by staking ETH or more USD for an U.S. Treasury bond they bought with USD.&#x20;

In Seasons, however, yield is paid in a diversified basket of liquid alternative assets, each with their unique volatility profiles and archetypes. This approach unlocks a novel amplifier for return potential and expands the protocol's implied utility for users (See: [How to Use Seasons](/seasons-docs/intro/introduction#how-to-use-seasons)).&#x20;

As of March 2026, distributions happen semi-weekly (twice per week). Payout assets are sent directly to the node's wallet address, eliminating any further cost or hassle of claiming yield payouts.&#x20;

{% hint style="warning" %}
&#x20;The distribution frequency may change in future as the protocol matures.
{% endhint %}

***

### TTT APY

While the TTT module doesn't have an inherent APY rate, the network-wide value generation can be summed up with the following formula:&#x20;

> `(Volume x`` `<sub>*`txFee`*</sub>`Rate) ÷ Time`&#x20;

{% hint style="success" icon="corn" %}
Examples of yield generated at different volumes:&#x20;

1. In 365 days, at $1,000,000 in volume x 10% fee = $100,000 in yield.
2. In 365 days, at $10,000,000 in volume x 10% fee = $1,000,000 in yield.&#x20;
3. In 365 days, at $100,000,000 in volume x 10% fee = $10,000,000 in yield.&#x20;
4. In 365 days, at $1,000,000,000 in volume x 10% fee = $100,000,000 in yield.
   {% endhint %}

Given Seasons' unique nuance where a Node is any address with a qualifying balance (i.e., 10,000+ $SEAS) — and qualifying balances are based on relative weight — individual APY depends on several constituent variables:

1. Rolling Volume
2. <sub>*txfee*</sub>Rate (Buy)
3. <sub>*txfee*</sub>Rate (Sell)
4. Node Cost (at the time of entry)
5. Relative NodeMap
6. Yield (assets)
7. Yield (strategy)
8. Time

{% hint style="info" %}
See [APY](/seasons-docs/protocol/apy) for more details on how APY is calculated within Seasons' yield mechanism.&#x20;
{% endhint %}

### Security

Security defines a protocol's trustworthiness. Because no matter how good a potential opportunity may seem, it holds no value unless its secure.&#x20;

Seasons thus implements multi-pronged security/safety parameters: *threshold triggers*, *shuffling escrows*, and *failover buffers*. In addition to conducting multiple independent audits for each smart contract, Seasons has taken preemptive measures to adopt a security-first design with guardrails at every possible vector across processes, such as:&#x20;

* **No TVL/Management/Ownership:** Seasons does not own/posses any pools or manage any assets, thereby reducing counterpart risk and removing the surface area of assets that can be swiped in the event of private key leaks or internal agent conflict.
* **Yield Escrow (Acquirer/Distributor) Shuffling:** Each Season has its own dedicated distributor address assigned, and sometimes even multiple distributors.
* **Acquirer Slippage Stops:** Slippage limits are set to ranges reflecting an asset’s category bracket (0.05%–0.25% for Blue Chips, 0.5%–1.5% for Undedogs, and 1%-5% for Rising Stars), with buffer space for moments of congestion.

# SSYM

Stakeholder Stablecoin Yield Module

{% hint style="warning" %}
🚧 **NOT YET ACTIVE** 🚧\
*The SSYM shall be the second module in the Seasons Yield 3.0 system by order of implementation. After backtesting, experimental pilot programs will be conducted prior to the SSYM's instantiation. Tentatively anticipating an alpha version to be live before Q4 of 2026.*
{% endhint %}

Slow, steady, and reliable, the SSYM is one of the three value drivers within the Seasons yield mechanism. Its consistent, albeit low, rate of return functions as the lower bound/base source of positive value pressure into the Seasons mechanism.&#x20;

<figure><img src="https://714818086-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2FLy6pIqxkZG5ykeDMj55p%2Fuploads%2FNt43ekg08BlPqy1BCjX6%2FSSYM2.png?alt=media&amp;token=c4f64d57-642d-41c5-8f08-a1f62deecafb" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

### **Basic Structure**

A pool of proceeds is directed towards the accumulation of stablecoins (USDt, USDc, et al.). Those stablecoins are then deposited into the most secure, overcollateralized lending protocols, including, but not limited to, Jupiter and Kamino. The generated stablecoin returns are regularly injected into three tracks of the SEASONS yield mechanism, which APY returns, increase the pool's own return rate, and stimulate token velocity.

### **How does it work?**

Trading the $SEAS token incurs a fee. Fees are generated and accounted for in $SEAS tokens at the moment of a transaction. Those fees are secured in stablecoins and deposited into the YMS. The YMS then lends out the stablecoins while they transition from yield generation to yield payout. During the period of time that they are deposited, they accrue the market-average rate of return on lending (\~1-7% APY). Once the moment of the next yield distribution arrives, the deposited amount is withdrawn, minus the profits, and distributed among the nodes. Profits are then pushed through the SEASONS network.

<figure><img src="https://714818086-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2FLy6pIqxkZG5ykeDMj55p%2Fuploads%2FLpmfNlbzGtqQRj2LqMYW%2FYield%20System_2.png?alt=media&amp;token=d9407425-4bf6-4582-9aba-63657ee2786d" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

### **Proceed Utilization**

The SSYM utilizes its proceeds across three tracks. Given the variance of impact that each individual track provides, the exact weights of each track are subject to optimization ad hoc.

**1) Yield Boosting** \
&#x20; *% is distributed with the yield distribution (apropos)*\
**2)** **Reserve Strengthening**\
&#x20; *% used as a compounding vector to accelerate the absolute size of SSYM reserves*\
**3) $SEAS BuyBack**\
&#x20; *% used to accumulate $SEAS from the open market*&#x20;

#### 1) Yield Boosting

Amplification of the total size of an individual yield distribution. This provides a smooth, small increase to each individual payout, which in turn bolsters the rate of return that a user receives.

#### 2) Reserve Strengthening

Acceleration of the SSYM absolute reserve size. The impact of the SSYM on the SEASONS yield mechanism is linear, directly related to its size. The larger the SSYM, the more yield it generates, and the more it attracts larger market participants.

#### 3) $SEAS Buyback

A dual service function that acquires $SEAS tokens on the open markets and removes them from circulation. The SSYM acts as a vector exerting constant positive pressure on the $SEAS token, simultaneously removing supply from orderbooks/liquidity pools while generating volume (that ultimately boosts the TTT yield module).

### **Controls**

The SSYM belongs to the $SEAS token holders and is owned/controlled by the Seasons DAO.&#x20;

In the earlier stages of the project, during the establishment of the yield mechanism and prior to the DAO's instantiation, the SSYM will require agility in its application and bootstrapping; thus, it shall not be influenced by the node networks.&#x20;

Once controls are fully handed over to the DAO, token holders shall be given the ability to adjust the functionality of the SSYM as they find fit, with rights ranging from the redirection of capital flow to the ability to halt it.

### **Security**

Given the passive liquid nature of the SSYM, it becomes an object of interest to malicious actors, internally and externally (node operators and general industry participants); thus, protective measures are instituted to preemptively mitigate subversive takeover/draining attempts.

The SSYM has no single point of operations; rather, it is a compendium of multiple independent, unrelated addresses working in orchestration. The exact address being interacted with at any moment regularly cycles amongst other addresses.&#x20;

In the event a DAO decision passes to halt the SSYM, the reserves remain untouched for a preliminary period while the mechanism adjusts to its new rhythm. After the halt period elapses, another vote must be passed to deactivate and dismantle the SSYM, which will, by default, incur a prolonged emission process that ultimately deters short-term operators.

# YAV

Yield Asset Vaults

{% hint style="warning" %}
🚧 **NOT YET ACTIVE** 🚧\
*The YAV is the third and final module in the Seasons Yield 3.0 system by order of implementation. With a wider area of application and the necessity of a dedicated orchestration layer for strategy management, the YAV component is expected to enter experimentation after the SSYM's proper integration.*

One of the three core modules in the Seasons Yield 3.0 mechanism, the YAV is a smart contract that automatically deploys assets into a mixture of onchain DEFI strategies. As the module with the highest degree of technical complexity/malleability in its implementation, the YAV provides variable amplification of yield payouts.

<figure><img src="https://714818086-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2FLy6pIqxkZG5ykeDMj55p%2Fuploads%2FzS9jbUa2ftBqx6tR5RBA%2FYAV2.png?alt=media&amp;token=5861286d-4d9d-4d43-a1a9-4486a78b3ec8" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

### **Basic Structure**

A Vault consisting of Inclusion Set assets is instantiated that belongs to Seasons (Node) Token Holders. The vault routes its holdings through a predefined DEFI strategy circuit and directs proceeds back through the Seasons network. Made accessible to the general public on alternative DEFI platforms, as well as on the Seasons terminal; the vault charges a service fee which ultimately contributes the Yield 3.0 system's returns. The embedded reflexivity features contribute to the bottom-line sustainability by ensuring persistent growth rates.

The YAV utilizes its proceeds as follows:\
1\) Yield assets are accumulated by Seasons Network\
2\) Yield assets are paired/bundled into vaults\
3\) Vaults are set at competitive rates to attract as much trade volume through them as possible\
4\) During yield distributions, yield is pulled from vaults, and proceeds accumulated through LP are injected into the Seasons Yield mechanism.

### **YAV Composition**

The composition of the YAV is derived from the unique profiles of assets as they align with the Seasons core purpose of generating steady flows of revenue without systemic risks.&#x20;

0\) Prequalified by Seasons Inclusion Set Criteria\
1\) Reduce impacts of slippage\
2\) Reduce impacts of impermanent loss\
3\) Maximize for utilization/volume capture\
4\) Maximize for fee capture

In addition to possessing highly sought-after characteristics, namely liquidity, the assets must be maximally accessible and resilient against centralized counterparty failures.

Vaults are intended to be perpetual; once launched, disengaging vaults is avoided.

### **YAV Strategies**

Selected based on their risk profiles and return capabilities, the YAV leverages a multitude of variable DEFI strategies across the Solana blockchain ecosystem. With the surface area of opportunities constantly shifting, there are no more than 4 (four) strategies running simultaneously, each of which can be actively substituted.

Some of the Potential Strategies:\
\- Liquidity Provision\
\- Delta Neutral Fee Accumulation\
\- Lending\
\- Liquidations

### **Proceed Utilization**

The purpose of the YAV is twofold: first, to provide a boosted return on yield by allocating a fraction of its proceeds to each network distribution, and second, to grow its own reserve of assets.&#x20;

An earning period for the YAV is 72-96 hours, the time between distributions. During that block of time, the YAV generates revenue and, at the moment of the next distribution, flows across three tracks. Given the variability in impact that each individual track provides, the exact weights of each track are subject to ad hoc optimization.

**1) Yield Boosting** \
&#x20; *% is distributed with the yield distribution (apropos)*\
**2)** **Reserve Strengthening**\
&#x20; *% used as a compounding vector to accelerate the absolute size of YAV reserves*\
**3) $SEAS BuyBack**\
&#x20; *% used to accumulate $SEAS from the open market*&#x20;

#### 1) Yield Boosting

Steady, gradual injections of additional revenue to amplify the total size of an individual yield distribution. On top of the distribution included from the TTT and SSYM, the YAV bolsters the sum being sent; ultimately providing a smooth upwards pressure on user returns.

#### 2) Reserve Strengthening

Ensuring consistent lower-bound growth in the TVL owned by the Vault. The impact of the YAV on the Season yield mechanism is variable, dependent foremost on its size and the return potential of applied strategies. The larger the YAV reserves, the more yield it generates, and the more it attracts larger market participants.

#### 3) $SEAS Buyback

The second touchpoint for reflexive contribution to the Seasons ecosystem, the YAV spends the smallest fraction of its proceeds to exude consistent supportive open market pressure on the $SEAS token. While acquiring $SEAS from the liquidity pools and removing them from circulation, the activity triggers the TTT module, further bolstering the system's yield-generating capacity.<br>

### **Controls**

The YAV belongs to the $SEAS (node) token holders and is owned/controlled by the Seasons DAO. All of the vaults' self-contributed holdings constitute yield and thus belong to the network.&#x20;

The DAO/Node network influences which strategies are used, how proceeds are allocated and in what proportions, and may have the option to disengage vaults and reclaim the underlying assets.

In the earlier stages of the project, during the establishment of the yield mechanism and prior to the DAO's instantiation, the YAV will require agility in its application and bootstrapping; thus, it shall not be influenced by the node networks.&#x20;

# Distribution

Disseminating Yield

## Overview

Seasons leverages a novel system for delivering proceeds throughout the SEAS Network.&#x20;

Its emission configuration of where proceeds flow to, how often, what frequency value flows at, and in what form factor creates a flywheel effect on growth and sustainability; there are 2 technical factors that determine value flow, the {Node List} and the {Inclusion Set}.

#### **Epoch Node List&#x20;**<sup><sub>***(Where)***<sub></sup>

The Seasons Network recognizes Solana addresses that have become [Nodes](/seasons-docs/protocol/nodes) as value endpoints for receiving yield. By satisfying the system's requirements, nodes are registered in the active {Epoch Node List} and automatically begin to receive yield payouts directly in their associated wallet address. No need to 'claim' and no lock-ups. Whenever a distribution event is triggered, the YMS-Acquirer/Distribution will reference the {Epoch Node List} to determine where to direct the next batch of proceeds.

{% hint style="success" icon="triple-chevrons-right" %}
**Learn more about:**  [<kbd><mark style="color:blue;">**`Nodes`**<mark style="color:blue;"></kbd>](/seasons-docs/protocol/nodes)
{% endhint %}

#### **Inclusion Set&#x20;**<sup><sub>***(In what formfactor)***<sub></sup>

Seasons implements an "[Inclusion Set](/seasons-docs/protocol/yield-3.0-mechanism/distribution/inclusion-lists)" — a selection of liquid assets that have satisfied a [predefined qualitative criteria](/seasons-docs/protocol/yield-3.0-mechanism/distribution/inclusion-lists#inclusion-criteria) — to determine what tokens users receive in their wallets as yield. Whenever a distribution event is engaged, the YMS will transfer the epoch's revenue to an Acquirer/Distributor, which swap everything according to the specifications in its references {Inclusion Set} prior to sending it out.

{% hint style="success" icon="triple-chevrons-right" %}
**Learn more about:**  [<kbd><mark style="color:blue;">**`Inclusion Sets`**<mark style="color:blue;"></kbd>](/seasons-docs/protocol/yield-3.0-mechanism/distribution/inclusion-lists)
{% endhint %}

#### Emission Size <sup><sub>*(How much/APY)*<sub></sup>

Currently, the total value of yield distributed per round operates on a rolling two-day basis \[48 hours] worth of generated yield. For every 48-72-hour time period (based on the CET+1 time zone), the total yield generated is summed up and split into acquiring the inclusion set assets. The asset acquisition happens within a 24-hour time block of the actual distribution to avoid any significant shifts in price/value.

{% hint style="success" icon="triple-chevrons-right" %}
**Learn more about:**  [<kbd><mark style="color:blue;">**`APY`**<mark style="color:blue;"></kbd>](/seasons-docs/protocol/apy)
{% endhint %}

#### Emission Frequency <sup><sub>*(How often)*<sub></sup>

During its initial stage, Seasons' yield distribution frequency has been set to semi-weekly (twice per week). As of March 2026, distributions happen every Sunday and Wednesday. However, this is subject to adaptation ad hoc as the protocol evolves.

#### Epochs&#x20;

An Epoch refers to a 72-96-hour period of time that applies specifically to a given distribution. The Epoch specifies from which period of time it is claiming a yield payout, the list of all qualifying $SEAS node addresses, the aggregate token balance of all qualifying yield recipients, and the amounts of each output asset that was/is distributed.

## How does it work?

1. A Season is outlined {objective}
2. Potential group of Candidate/Desirable assets are aggregated into a list
3. Pre-emptive threshold filtration conducted to consolidate group into (8) that are in alignment
4. Qualifiers of Threshold move into full screening
5. Final results are reviewed and published as the "Active Inclusion Set"
6. The YMS references the "Active Inclusion Set" during every round of distribution

# Nodes

Network Owners and Operators

### What are Seasons Nodes?

Seasons Nodes are public Solana network wallet addresses that have satisfied the Yield 3.0 Mechanism's criteria and, in turn, become recognized by the Seasons network as operators. Operators are users that have proved their alignment with the Network's well-being through their fee contributions while entering a $SEAS position. By virtue of this contribution, Seasons Network endows them with operational and ownership rights.

***

### Functions & Capabilities

Nodes are seen and treated as Owners by the Seasons network; therefore, they are granted a substantial amount of influence over it, namely in the form of irrefutable claims on the capital flows. Becoming endpoints where the Yield 3.0 mechanism deposits its Liquidity in order to direct how/where the liquidity goes; Nodes can harvest the yield, compound their rates, or redirect it altogether.&#x20;

Ownership comes with responsibility. In Seasons, that responsibility is materialized in the form of stewardship. Shepherding the protocol to its healthiest possible version/state. Nodes constantly make decisions that directly influence the protocol's short- and long-term capacities and capabilities.&#x20;

#### **Asset Routing**&#x20;

As yield is dispersed throughout the network, nodes have the final decision on whether or not and how the Seasons yield mechanism handles liquidity. They can redirect flows immediately to help smooth out the mechanism, or harvest the proceeds for delayed deployment under the assumption the proceeds will grow independently. They can entirely deflect the flows and extract the value for external (independent/unrelated) purposes.

#### **Mechanism Configuration**

Influence elements of the Yield 3.0 system's operations. The Yield 3.0 Mechanism possesses a multitude of gauges and formulas that can be adjusted in order to augment the protocol's operations. From the service fee charged by the TTT, to the SSYM's proceeds weight utilization, to the YAV's compositions, and everything in between (even the distribution rates); roughly 40 elements are available for collaborative construction.&#x20;

#### **DAO membership**

All nodes possess the unique rights to contribute to the decision-making processes. Marketing initiatives, community efforts, strategic partnerships, technological development, treasury activation, resource acquisition, and many other critical vectors that must be considered for Seasons' growth and presence in the world are resolved collaboratively.&#x20;

#### **PoL Claims**

As owners of the mechanism's cash flow, Seasons Nodes are indirectly owners of the object(s) generating that cash flow. Nodes that have reached the highest alignment via contributions and time commitments satisfy the Yield Mechanism's final criteria and unlock proportionate withdrawal rights on the Protocol Owned Liquidity.

# APY

Determining Seasons Annual Percentage Yield Potential

While to some degree reliant on the overall sentiment of the economy *(as is any instrument, business, or industry)*, SEASONS APY is derived through an amalgamation of three (3) value-generating channels that are independent from one another. The driving forces of each channel imply distinct rate-of-return and risk profiles, in turn increasing variance and positioning the SEASONS Yield system apart from other solutions on the market.

* **TTT** - generation of revenue based on trading activity
* **SSYM** - generation of revenue based on industry lending rates
* **YAV** - generation of revenue based on fees accrued from the provision of liquidity.

Unlike the yield generated from traditional vehicles, SEASONS yield is derived from socio-economic vectors unrelated to government/legacy instruments; this breakage of dependency links introduces a new capacity for yield, one that is highly subjective, variable, and volatile.&#x20;

Given the multi-depth variances of not just the dynamic source of yield but the dynamism of the type of yield, Season's utilizes a novel Elastic framework to establish APY.

***

### Determining Node APY&#x20;

APY in Season's is established by aggregating the output revenue being generated by each of its yield channels across two dimensions: a Network's APY/YR and the Individual $SEAS node APY.

The best expression can be found in an empirical simulation as follows:

**Annualized distributions:** $3,500,000 + $650,000 of upwards pressure on value

* **TTT** - $3,000,000 distributions&#x20;
* **SSYM** - $200,000 distributions *+ $300,000 anscillary impact*
* **YAV** - $300,000 distributions *+ $350,000 anscillary impact*

**If TTT** facilitates $30,000,000 in volume and generates $3,000,000 in fees, of which the entire amount is distributed, then this channel produces $3,000,000 in annualized distributions.

**If SSYM** holdings $10,000,000 in USDc and generates $500,000, of which $200,000 is distributed as yield, $150,000 of which buys back $SEAS tokens *(contributing $15,000 in TTT revenue)*, and $150,000 of which goes to increasing the size of the SSYM, then this channel produces $200,000 in annualized distributions.

**If the YAV** has $10,000,000 in asset TVL and facilitates enough trade activity to generate $650,000 in annualized fees, of which $300,000 is distributed as yield, $175,000 of which buys back $SEAS tokens *(contributing $17,500 in TTT revenue)*, and $175,000 of which goes to increasing the YAV TVL, then this channel produces $300,000 in annualized distributions.

Under the assumption that there are 1,000 nodes, all of which entered at the same base cost of $5,000 and possess equal balances over time, then each node will receive an equal portion of the $3,500,000 or $3,500; and an APY of approximately \~70%. *(without factoring in the growth reserve and potential of token volatility).*

*The formula can be applied to fit any number of assumptions:*\
*( $ TTT*<sub>*dis +*</sub>*&#x20;$ SSYM*<sub>*dis +*</sub>*&#x20;$ YAV*<sub>*dis*</sub>*&#x20;) /* (Node/NodeSTATE) = APY of node<br>

#### Network APY\* (YR)

The network itself does not have an APY rate; it has a base YR (*yield generation rate)*. The Yield generation rate is a by-product of the total annual yield SEASONS distributes as measured against its market presence. The YR serves as a gauge for evaluating the well-being of the $SEAS token.

Building on the above assumptions of $3,500,000 in distributions and a base node entry spot price of $0.50 ($500m FDV), the YR can be established by simply dividing the FDV by the distributions and then dividing it again by 10 to arrive at a YR of ***14.28571428571429***

{% hint style="info" %}
**YR modeling:**

At ***$10,000,000*** distributions <sup>**x**</sup> ***$100,000,000*** FDV = YR **1**\
At ***$7,000,000*** distributions <sup>**x**</sup> ***$350,000,000*** FDV = YR **5**\
At ***$5,000,000*** distributions <sup>**x**</sup> ***$500,000,000*** FDV = YR **10**\
At ***$4,000,000*** distributions <sup>**x**</sup> ***$800,000,000*** FDV = YR **20**
{% endhint %}

Generally, the YR should be in the 5-25 range. Everything outside of that range would indicate a logical value discrepancy.

The lower the YR, the higher the value proposition to own a node and, in turn, the incentive to drive demand market activity of $SEAS. Inversely, the higher the YR, the more overheated the competition between nodes over the same pool of capital, incentivizing supply-driven market activity.

### Elastic APY&#x20;

The yield generated/distributed by Seasons is dynamic and highly susceptible to changes on a per-distribution basis.

There are five (5) factors for the estimation of yield:

* Total # of nodes + total # of $SEAS tokens on all nodes
* Relative epoch weight of reciever:total amount of epoch $SEAS tokens
* Volume of $SEAS transactions from applicable time block of yield payout
* Total # of stablecoins in SSYM + current lending rate
* Total # of Vault assets in YAV + current LP rate

Each one of these 5 factors is in constant flux, creating variance in a progressive climb amid a steady recursive compounding.

### Utility: <a href="#specifications" id="specifications"></a>

The $SEAS token utility can be summed up in three (3) points:

1. Access Yield
2. Owning Yield Flow (Becoming Node)
3. DAO membership\*

Stripped of complexity in order to streamline the effectiveness of its purpose: accessing and generating yield, the SEAS token's yield-generating properties specify that any address that the system recognizes as a Node qualifies for the next round of yield distribution.&#x20;

There are two ways for an address to be recognized as a Node: either the consumer path or the operator path.&#x20;

#### Accessing Yield

The consumer path is a short-lived, singular inclusion into the Node set, whereby an address must acquire $SEAS off the open market and burn it. As soon as an address burns the $SEAS, it is added into the upcoming distribution mold and receives a portion of the distribution. This function treats a single burn transaction as minimal node weight or exactly 10,000 tokens. This function can be called once per address per round of yield distribution. Conducting consecutive burns within a single distribution window does not register.&#x20;

#### Owning Yield

The operator path of becoming a node means indefinitely plugging into the yield system and generating proceeds in perpetuity by holding the threshold amount of \[ 10,000 ] $SEAS tokens.

Logic:

* <sup><sub>Holding over 10,000 (10k) tokens \[1/100,000th total supply] signals adequate contribution and activates passive yield system.<sub></sup>
* <sup><sub>Dex trading incurs set entry and exit fees \[10% of transaction] in the form of slippage during any open market BUY/SELL.<sub></sup>
* <sup><sub>Fees are collected into an "Acquirer" smart contract with two internal triggers that will swap holdings into {<sub></sup><sup><sub>*Active\_Inclusion\_List*<sub></sup><sup><sub>} when triggered.<sub></sup>
* <sup><sub>After swapping, assets are deposited in “Escrow-Distribution” smart contract to activate {<sub></sup><sup><sub>*\_distribute\_funds.exe*<sub></sup><sup><sub>} and disburse them proportionately to all qualifying addresses.<sub></sup>

When $SEAS tokens are allocated, their movement triggers the transfer fee function, which redirects 10% to the YMS. The YMS is tasked with filtration and identification of such flows to compartmentalize the transaction resulting impact; either receiving an "operational" tag for temporary partial custody of the supply supplementing (which does not spread the flow into yield) or as a market transaction (which does get pushed to yield).

This same logic applies to locking tokens.

Once tokens arrive at their holding addresses, nominated accounts then lock and remove some amount from circulation. When implementing a lock, a transfer is required, in turn activating the fee. After locks mature, they return their $SEAS tokens to the token account of the locking address; once again, invoking a 10% fee. These fees are captured by the YMS and are classified as operational; at the moment of a supply unlock, the YMS will inject the fees back into its originating source. *They do not contribute to yield generation/distribution.*

## Emissions:

With all 1,000,000,000 minted into circulation at launch and the mint authority revoked, Seasons leverages a multitude of chronologically varying lockers to manage the $SEAS token supply.

Lockers, belonging to specific causes, periodically mature and release $SEAS back to their originating destinations; once matured, the state/degree of need for the $SEAS token at that moment in time (based on historic trails and near-term assumptions) is assessed, and in the event that the tokens are found to be of no critical service, they are once again relocked. However, if $SEAS tokens are determined to be an important tool, then only a necessary portion of tokens is retained and the remainder relocked.

# Strategic Repository

Ideas on using Seasons Yield

{% hint style="warning" %}
The below strategies are merely ***suggestions*** and should be treated at such; this is not an endorsement or encouragement for the public to replicate; these are simply strategies that have proven effective during simulation and retroactive testing.
{% endhint %}

Due to the unique design of the Season's system, it can be an excellent alternative/addition to a balanced, diversified portfolio strategy. While there are numerous potential implementations that can augment returns beyond traditional tools, the options provided below are varied based on risk, return potential, and degree of user involvement.&#x20;

These strategies are based on the fundamental potential operation and *do not* factor in the price of the $SEAS token! Users should make their own assumptions on how any given strategy will augment their yield.

Each strategy is accompanied by a description and 4 explainer vectors that evaluate *concept*, *impact*, *time preference*, and *management*. Each strategy can be implemented in isolation or combined with others to further increase the variability of its impact.\
\
**Concept**: Simple explanation of the Strategy\
**Impact**: How much the strategy can affect the APY\
**Time Preference:** How long a user expects to/can be involved\
**Management**: The amount of manual/active involvement necessary from the user's side to implement.<br>

***

### 1️⃣ Strategy 1️⃣ : "Yield-Bonding"

**Concept:** Hold on to assets generated via Yield\
**Impact:** Heavy\
**Time preference:** Long/Undetermined\
**Management:** Near none, passive monitoring\
\
**Description:** Most effective for those looking to leverage a speculative informational edge, build a portfolio, or those with prolonged time horizons. Colloquially referred to as "Yield-bonding"; the first strategy is a simple *set-it-and-forget-it* method with exponential impact on the APY of a $SEAS Seasons holder's portfolio. Yield Bonding adds the additional variables of dynamisms inherent to the assets generated through yield; binding the user's APY to the value of the yield; if the yield grows in value, so does the user's APY, likewise, in the event the assets devalue, the user's APY moves in tandem.

### 2️⃣Strategy 2️⃣:  "Yield-Locking"

**Concept:** Sell yielded assets off into Stablecoins\
**Impact:** Low\
**Time preference:** Short/Immediate\
**Management:** Low planned engagement

**Description:** The most conservative approach for $SEAS yield. By converting all generated yield at a set cadence (once per day) or immediately upon receiving it, the APY becomes somewhat more linear, enabling a higher degree of predictability in a portfolio. By muting volatility, "Yield-Locking" becomes an excellent option for users intent on building cash reserves or as a source of spending resources.&#x20;

### **3️⃣ Strategy 3️⃣ : "Yield-Compounding"**

**Concept:** Swap Yield into $SEAS\
**Impact:** Exponential\
**Time preference:** Long/Undetermined\
**Management:** Active & Passive <sup>*(\*auto-function activation at user discretion, planned development)*</sup> \
\
**Description:** Accelerant/amplifier of $SEAS Yield system, best for users looking to augment the rate of yield accrual. As the name might suggest, Yield Compounding is a strategy focused on growing the momentum at which yield is generated. By increasing the $SEAS position itself, the relative weight of a user/address grows and, in turn, captures a larger portion of yield.&#x20;

### 4️⃣ **Strategy** 4️⃣ **: "Yield-Speculation"**

**Concept:** Swap Yield into other Assets\
**Impact:** Exponential\
**Time preference:** Short/Immediate\
**Management:** Hyper-active

**Description:** Effectively a way to de-risk by putting earned yield directly to work. As a managed source to fund trading activities, whereby yield turns into a tool to amplify speculative gains. This is not a direct way to augment APY, it is a way to augment a total financial strategy.

# Applying Yield 3.0

Putting Seasons Protocol to Work

***

The surface area of application for the Seasons protocol (Yield 3.0 mechanism), by virtue of its liquid nature, overlaps multiple industry segments; from the DEFI spectrum of treasury and portfolio management, to the TradFI savings vehicle, commercial B2B rewards programs, Agentic behavior tools, and more in between.&#x20;

Subject to the creative whims of its required use case, ultimately the Seasons Yield 3.0 mechanism complements and boosts all existing user value flows. The protocol's market productization will expand as new use cases are discovered and the product becomes more resilient, efficient, and recognized.

***

#### 1️⃣ HYSA - High Yield Savings Account

The passive management, pay-to-play configuration, and risk profile of a node in the portfolio of an operator positions the node as a HYSA *(High Yield Savings Account)*.&#x20;

A digitally/crypto native, end-to-end tokenized, liquid, fungible, fractionalized cashflow system directed at the generation, distribution, and accumulation of revenue for the burgeoning agentic internet economy.

Own a node, own the system's cash flow, and receive distributions regularly.

The $SEAS token represents membership to a network that redistributes transactional fee rewards proportionately amongst its holders. Once any on-chain address possesses the threshold minimum of 10,000 $SEAS tokens, it is automatically inducted into the "node list" and begins collecting a portion of transaction fees pro rata.&#x20;

Modern Yield systems offer either sub-optimal APYs, below the growing costs of living, or demand a high degree of active management *(which results in either management fees that eat away the surpluses to begin with or cost the user in time/attention).*&#x20;

The Seasons Yield mechanism has taken into account the reality of yield systems and, in turn, tapped into the most profitable, sustainable, resilient business models in the financial world to source the generation, capturing, and distribution of yield.

#### 2️⃣ Portfolio Auto-builder

<sub>***Acquire, Set, and allow Seasons to build your Portfolio.***</sub> \ <sup>***- Grow, Adapt, Diversify.***</sup>

Based on an adaptation of principles from the DCA (Dollar Cost Averaging) model *(which remains the kingmaker trading/investing approach for retail, professionals, and venture capital alike),* Seasons becomes an automated vehicle with which users can build portfolios ***without having to repeatedly deploy capital.***

Coupling that capacity with the exotic/uncommon nature of the assets accumulated via distributions and the deep due diligence that Season's does in order to adhere strictly to its QA policies whenever considering which assets are included for distribution, Users are able to offload the cognitive analytical burden to Season's Inclusion models.

#### 3️⃣ Asset Distribution Pipeline

<sub>***On-Chain, Post-Token-Launch, B2B***</sub>

Projects of all sizes face the eternal quest to drive more attention, interest, and demand for their digital assets; this consumes and influences their day-to-day activity. For projects at earlier stages or with less funding, this fundamental business demand detracts from their focus on product; for later-stage projects, this leads to a standstill/slowdown as an organic upper bound is achieved.&#x20;

Defaulting to some mixture of parasitic marketing models that include smashing keyboards on social media, hiring outsourced marketing teams, and engaging extractive counterparties; the industry is starved of an adequate product-based solution.

Stimulating discovery by growing the on-chain footprint of every digital token that interacts with it, Seasons is the ideal (post-launch) component in any token-related project's market presence. Providing a steady stream of swaps through a community of mature/higher value holders/on-chain addresses that contributes to a project's volume by directing a portion of the fee-based rewards through their assets.

<sub>***Reflexive auto-imbued incentive mechanism***</sub>

### Purpose

The Seasons DAO serves as a community form for network members who possess notable impact/weight to coordinate and collaborate. It is a communication platform dedicated to the Yield 3.0 mechanism, where the system's power users make product-defining decisions.

Ultimately, the DAO inherits control over the yield mechanism, becoming the steward and guardian of the yield 3.0 system.&#x20;

### Functionality

The DAO shall be able to submit, vote on, and discuss SEAips (Season Improvement Proposals) as they pertain to the mechanical configuration of the Seasons Yield System. Enabling users to direct the flows of capital, alter distribution frequencies, calibrate fees, and then experience the outcome of their decision-making, unlocks a deeper bond and, in turn, alignment between users and the Yield 3.0 mechanism's well-being.

The DAO will have autonomy over every individual element of each module within the Seasons Yield system; allowing the members to intimately influence how the system works. A shorthand list of examples is as follows:

* Enable “asset nomination” <sup>*{community selected inclusion sets}*</sup>
* Enable “delisting requests” <sup>*{community selected inclusion sets}*</sup>
* Enable “TTT Fee range” <sup>*{adjustment of fee size}*</sup>&#x20;
* Enable “TTT Fee gauge” <sup>*{adjustment of fee allocation}*</sup>&#x20;
* Enable “SSYM Fee range” <sup>*{adjustment of fee size}*</sup>&#x20;
* Enable “SSYM Fee gauge” <sup>*{adjustment of fee allocation}*</sup>&#x20;
* Enable “Distribution triggers” <sup>*{adjustment of when/how assets are distributed}*</sup>
* Enable “Inclusion Set Refinements” <sup>*(adjustment of proportions & number of assets included)*</sup>
* Enable “Node Inclusion List"  <sup>*{expand or contract accessiblity}*</sup>
* Enable “YMS behavior"  <sup>*{strategic acquisition/distribution schemes)*</sup>
* et al.

### Requirements&#x20;

By virtue of the Seasons Yield system design, where $SEAS (node) token holders are the owners of the system's yield flow, the capacity to steer the protocol in the right direction should belong to them, as the incentives are aligned with the best progression of the mechanism.

Thus, becoming a DAO member for the Seasons Yield Mechanism is a straightforward process of becoming a node. Every Node (wallet with a qualifying balance above 10,000 $SEAS tokens) is automatically inducted into the DAO and given participation rights.

### DAO Power

DAO power is a direct expression of how much influence a member possesses. Power distribution within the Seasons DAO happens pro rata to a member's Node weight on a 1-token-1-unit of power.

The more DAO power a user has, the more votes they have.

Other potential models will be explored, including a time weight factor and a contribution factor.

### Proposals

Seasons DAO proposals are independently submitted requests that suggest some kind of operational/technical adaptation to the Yield Mechanism in pursuit of potential improvements.

All DAO members can read and vote on proposals; however, only Nodes above 100,000 $SEAS tokens are given the right to submit a proposal.

Every proposal is submitted with a 250 $SEAS token bond; if a proposal is denied, the bond is removed from circulation. If a proposal is approved, then the bonded $SEAS are returned to the proposer.

Proposals can be in 1 of 4 states:\
*- Implemented*\
*- Approved*\
*- Denied*\
*- In Revision*

*\* Proposals that are resubmitted incur an additional 50 $SEAS tokens for each resubmission.*&#x20;

# Swap tokens on DEX

Exchange tokens into your wallet using decentralized exchange (DEX).

*\*  This guide assumes the reader already has a Solana wallet address. If not, please refer to the Season's Solana Wallet Creation Guide or any publicly available resources online.*&#x20;

*\*\*  This guide is for people who have their assets in self-custody on-chain.*

*\*\*\* If you are using a centralized solution that custodies assets, such as an Exchange (such as ByBit) or wallet (such as CoinBase), then you will be able to request a withdrawal to the network of your choice. Here, you would simply select \[Solana or SPL], input your Solana address, and receive your funds as you would with any asset on any other network.*

{% hint style="warning" %} <sup>*\*\*\*\* The recommended solutions provided here are not endorsements of the platforms or their tokens. SEASONS recommendations are for informational purposes only, and Seasons is not responsible for any unintentional consequences from the use of the named platforms.*</sup>
{% endhint %}

***

#### Quick Start Guide: <a href="#quick-start-guide" id="quick-start-guide"></a>

1. Find DEX
2. Open & connect wallet&#x20;
3. Select "Selling" & "Buying" asset, enter amount.&#x20;
4. Make swap & confirm transaction
5. Check balance&#x20;

## **Written Guide** <a href="#quick-start-guide" id="quick-start-guide"></a>

### **Step 1: Choose a DEX Platform**

Find a **DEX application** compatible with the **Solana network**.\
Given the widespread adoption of Solana, nearly every tier-1 provider supports it. Many DEXs now offer **in-chain functionality** directly from their dashboards, making trading seamless and efficient.

The best DEX ultimately comes down to **personal preference** — whether based on recommendations from trusted sources or your own research. Most major providers offer highly competitive rates and experiences.

> ✅ **Best practice:** Maintain 2–3 trusted DEX options to ensure flexibility. Some may offer faster transactions, others better liquidity or lower fees.

**The Seasons team recommends the following Solana DEXs:**

* [**Jupiter**](https://jup.ag/)
* [**Raydium**](https://raydium.io/)
* [**Orca**](https://www.orca.so/)

***

### **Step 2: Access Your Wallet and Connect to the DEX**

1. Open your **self-custodial Solana wallet** (e.g., **Phantom**, **Solflare**, or similar).
2. Click the **“Discover”** 🔎 button.
3. In the search bar, type the DEX’s address (for example, `jup.ag`) and open it.
4. Once on the DEX platform, click **Connect Wallet** and approve the connection request in your wallet window.

> 💡 **Tip:**
>
> * On **mobile**, you’ll operate entirely within the wallet application.
> * On **desktop or browser**, you’ll use a **wallet extension** to connect.

Each DEX has a slightly different design, but the **core functionality remains the same** across platforms.

***

### **Step 3: Select Trading Assets**

1. Under the **“Selling”** field, choose the crypto or asset you wish to **sell**.
2. Under the **“Buying”** field, choose the crypto or token you want to **buy**.
3. Enter the **amount** you wish to swap.
4. If the token does not appear in the list, find it using its **contract address**:
   * Copy the official token contract address.
   * Paste it into the DEX’s search bar to locate the token.

{% hint style="warning" %}
*Some DEXs may allow **multi-chain swaps or bridging** during the trade.*\
*For the most reliable execution and rates, keep both the **source and destination networks** the same (i.e., stay within Solana).*
{% endhint %}

***

### **Step 4: Review and Confirm**

After verifying that the **selected assets and amounts** are correct:

1. Click **Swap**.
2. Approve the transaction in the **wallet window** that appears.

Once confirmed, the DEX will execute the swap and display a success notification.

***

### **Step 5: Verify Your Updated Balance**

When the transaction is complete:

1. Reopen your wallet.
2. Check your updated **token balance**.

If you don’t immediately see the new token, enable or add it manually using the token’s **contract address**.

# Yield 101

## Yield&#x20;

Yield is the single most important element of a healthy financial system. It is the foundation upon which the modern economy is built; ***but what is it?***&#x20;

### What is Yield?

At the Highest level, Yield is the value earned/accumulated above the investment cost of an instrument over the course of time. It is a return premium on resources over time allocated to any arbitrary use cases/entities.

{% hint style="info" %}
**Example:**\
A Government is fundraising from the general public via bonds for telecommunication infrastructure expansions.

<sup>*Bond face value of a bond is $10,000 (redemption from government)*</sup>\ <sup>*Bonds Mature 10 years after the date they are purchased/"activated".*</sup>\ <sup>*Bonds are Auctioned at $9,000. (discount to their redemption value)*</sup>

That means the government believes it will be able to complete its task, recoup & profit from the gathered resources within 10 years enough to pay back all investors with a premium as a "thank you" for locking up some of their funds with the government.

In this case, if a person buy a Bond for $9,000 and in 10 years will be able to collect $10,000, their yield is $1,000. In percentage terms, it would be: \ <sup><sub><mark style="color:orange;">**(Total Profit / Initial investment)**<mark style="color:orange;"><sub></sup>\ <sup><sub><mark style="color:blue;">**($1,000 / $9,000 )**<mark style="color:blue;"><sub></sup> \ <mark style="color:$success;">**+**</mark><mark style="color:$success;">**&#x20;**</mark>*<mark style="color:$success;">**11.11%**</mark>*

That 11.11% is then amortized linearly across 10 years bringing at average APY of 1.11%\ <sup>*\*\*\* this example does not take into account variables of buying power or inflation, it is intended for illustrative purposes only.*</sup>
{% endhint %}

### Yield Before Yield

Civilizations have tapped the power of yield for several millennia as a core essence of social dynamics and ancient economics. The Etymology of the term itself, Yield, comes from Old English & Germanic roots, which translated directly means *"to pay, reward/ render, serve".* In fact, Yield itself stems from beyond finance as a whole. In the world of agriculture, yield is used by farmers to refer to the harvest of crops they will collect in a given season. In the days of Kings, yield was synonymous with plunder, the spoils of their warfare.

The principle is universal and timeless, profits as it relates to an investment over the course of time.

*Yield* is *Interest* is *ROI* is *Rewards*.

Which brings us to the days of modern man.

### Yield in The Modern Age

As the information age washed over the world and digitization swallowed society, value has been radically rehypothecated; meanings have lost their origins and almost entirely become reappropriated around money.

Banking has captured the imaginations/fantasy's of society and calibrated Yield entirely into the world of finance. Yield has become a very unsexy subject matter spoken of only in the circles of elites or sovereign nations. Most people remain either unaware or unattracted to the "yields" provided by traditional instrumentation such as government bonds.

If we overly simplify yield into the "cost of money over time"; we find that it exists in two vectors:

*- Lending/Borrowing*\
*- Funding Rates*

Lending and borrowing is the base foundation of it all, where money that is lent generates yield from the borrower who pays for access to that capital.&#x20;

Yield in the internet economy remains one of the most elusive elements of finance; before blockchain technology, it was essentially non-existent.

### Yield in Web3

Regardless of what narratives may attempt to superimpose into the public's consciousness, blockchain is at its core an autonomous triple-entry accounting system; a DLT divinely perfect transactional record; a financial tool.\
\
Rapidly approaching its second decade in public consciousness, and iterating through many versions, philosophies, and the latest generation of blockchain/DLT ushered in a new era of developmental capabilities through a confluence of social readiness and infrastructural capabilities that have unlocked previously unfathomable logical primitives.&#x20;

The nascent, early stage of crypto and its countless regulatory hurdles have imposed challenges on the industry which have held it back from fully being able to capture opportunities for yield.  Mavericks of open finance have stepped forward with innovative pseudo-reg-tech solutions to push the envelope of creativity, introducing a wholly new class of finance. <sub>*(including Pendle)*</sub>

{% hint style="success" %}
There is a fundamental difference in between crypto-based yield and Real Yield; one is hot air and the other doesn't need crypto to exist.\
\
**Yield**: Value that is generated (captured/distributed) in a projects native token via inflation or some variation of unbalanced incentives allocation.\
\
**Real Yield:** Value that is captured/distributed in liquid assets; these tend to have a foundation in the greater world economy such as government T-Bills.
{% endhint %}

Crypto/DEFI APY's have a muddled, albeit short, history which has made it infamous for unsustainable economic designs. The main culprits are low-fidelity attempts by the builders at arbitrage time through inflationary token models, in which case the teams themselves, or sophisticated market participants, exploit the less-informed populations.

Vaults provide unique earning opportunities by encapsulating a wider set of operations with varying risk-reward profiles. Vaults are composed of assets in which Seasons distributes yield and belongs to the $SEAS (node) token holders, accruing proceeds for injection into the network.\ <sub>*More details available in "*</sub> [<sub>*YAV*</sub> ](/seasons-docs/protocol/yield-3.0-mechanism/generation/yav)<sub>*" section of this documentation.*</sub><br>

<mark style="color:orange;">**\***</mark>**&#x20;YMS - Yield Management System**\
The YMS or Yield Management System is an orchestration layer that handles the entire lifecycle of yield in Seasons Yield 3.0 mechanism. The YMS directs and sequences the inputs and outputs between different system components. Coordinating the generation of proceeds with the distribution of them, acting as the oracle to reference the Inclusion and Node sets.\ <sub>*More details available in "*</sub> [<sub>*YMS*</sub> ](/seasons-docs/protocol/yield-3.0-mechanism)<sub>*" section of this documentation.*</sub><br>

<mark style="color:orange;">**\***</mark><mark style="color:blue;">**\***</mark> **Epoch**\
Epoch is a general term used to describe a pre-defined block of time. Many companies, projects, and protocols use Epochs to highlight product or system features. Seasons utilizes the term Epoch to refer to a round of distribution, specifically the total balance of $SEAS tokens on receiving nodes.<br>

### Node Weights

The weight of a Node represents its relative claim size as a percentage allocation of the yield distribution, determining the portion attributable to it at that distribution event. A Node's weight can, will, and usually does fluctuate from distribution event to distribution event.

Weight is determined by:

1. Adding together the $SEAS token balances of all active nodes
2. Dividing any individual node's balance by the aggregate sum

This ratio establishes the relative proportions of a distribution. The larger the weight, the larger the portion of a distribution.

The passive management, pay-to-play configuration, and risk profile of a node in the portfolio of an operator position the node as a HYSA (High Yield Savings Account).&#x20;

### FAQs - HYSA

<mark style="background-color:yellow;">**Q: What does HYSA mean??**</mark>\
**A:** *HYSA is the acronym that stands for **High-Yield Savings Account.***

<mark style="background-color:yellow;">**Q: How much does a HYSA generate?**</mark>\
**A:** *It varies on a per-payout basis, currently targeting \~>12% APY.*

<mark style="background-color:yellow;">**Q: What is the TTT Rate?**</mark>\
**A:** *The current tax rate for a transaction is 10%.*&#x20;

<mark style="background-color:yellow;">**Q: Can the TTT Rate change?**</mark>\
**A:** *Yes. The rate can be adjusted to anything the SEASON Node Network deems to be ideal. Adaptation of the rate becomes a consideration after the instantiation of the DAO.*

<mark style="background-color:yellow;">**Q: Are all transactions subject to TTT?**</mark>\
**A:** *Yes, every action involving the movement of $SEAS tokens from point A to point B triggers the TTT, including swaps (buy/sell), transfers (send/receive), and all smart contract invocation (escrow, provide LP, or locking).*

<mark style="background-color:yellow;">**Q: What happens with fees accumulated during transfers?**</mark>\
**A:** *Whenever $SEAS tokens are taxed on a transfer, they do not generate fees. Having no economic significance to the network, those tokens are recycled through the YMS (Yield Management System).*

**Q: What is the potential APY of a node?**\
A: Subjective and circumstantial; the APY of a node is determined by a multitude of factors acting in orchestration with each other. Seasons aims to achieve consistency within its target range of 6.58%-21.07%; however, variance outside of it is to be expected.

**Q: Should I split my Node if it has over 10,000 tokens?**\
It is a matter of preference and wallet management. All nodes are treated equally. Whether it is one address with 20,000 tokens or 2 addresses with 10,000 tokens each, they will generate equally proportional yield.

**Q: Will the requirement ever change?**\
Potentially by community consensus only after the DAO has been fully integrated.

**Q: Can I sell my tokens and still generate yield?**\
Yes, so long as the balance of your wallet is above 10,000 $SEAS, you will continue generating yield.

**Q: What assets do NODEs generate?**\
That depends on the "[Inclusion List](/seasons-docs/protocol/yield-3.0-mechanism/distribution/inclusion-lists)" at the time of acquisition/distribution.
